About Us

Our goal:


It is to develop the software that people want for Unix and Unix-like systems.



Our Mission:


With our first project, Grass GFX, our goal is to simplify application development by sparing people the trouble of dealing with complex and cumbersome graphics libraries (such as GDI, Win32, WPF, and the DirectX family).



Our Vision:


To develop libraries and programs that enable people to use or develop Unix and Unix-like systems more easily.



The Philosophy and Architecture of Our Project


In our projects, we maintain full control using pure C without resorting to unnecessary abstractions, proceeding according to our principles of being practical, minimal, and functional.
